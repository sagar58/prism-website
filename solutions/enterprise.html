THE REPUTATION CHALLENGE

Brands don't have a listening problem.

Every enterprise already tracks mentions, reviews and social conversation. What's harder is knowing which of those mentions are becoming something more — a narrative that spreads, hardens and starts shaping how customers, investors and employees see the brand.

The difficult part is separating an isolated complaint from a forming narrative, and organic criticism from coordinated activity designed to look organic.

The question is not how many people are talking. It is whether the conversation is turning into a narrative.
