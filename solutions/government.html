FROM INFORMATION TO GOVERNANCE INTELLIGENCE
THE INFORMATION CHALLENGE

Governments don’t have a visibility problem.

They have more information than ever — across news, social platforms, languages, regions, institutions and constantly changing public conversations.

The difficult part is identifying what matters inside that volume, understanding how separate developments connect, and recognising when a conversation is beginning to move in a consequential direction.

The question is not what is being said. It is what the information is becoming.
